What to remember

Signature = proof that the owner of the private key wrote this exact data. Change one character and it breaks.
Nonce = a counter inside every payment. An old signed payment cannot be played twice.
Balance check = the ledger refuses what you cannot afford, even with a perfect signature.
Trust = here you trust one server to apply the rules honestly. A blockchain spreads that job over many computers.
Signatures are real secp256k1 ECDSA (same as Ethereum), computed in your browser. Only your public key, address, nickname and signed payments are sent to the ledger — never your words or private key.
