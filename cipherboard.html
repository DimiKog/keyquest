How it works (30 seconds)
  1. You get a key pair. The public key can be shared; the private key never leaves your browser.
  2. To write to Bob you encrypt with Bob's public key (ECIES: secp256k1 key agreement + AES-256-GCM) — only Bob's private key can open it.
  3. You also sign it with your private key so Bob knows it is from you and was not altered.
  4. The encrypted packet goes on the public board. Anyone can see it; anyone can try to open it — and fail.
The server (if any) only ever stores ciphertext. Note that sender and recipient addresses are visible: encryption hides the content, not who talks to whom.
